Our story

We started where you are.

Practice Proof began with a simple observation: professional practices were being underserved by a marketing industry that didn't understand them. Agencies arrived with templates. They sent junior account managers. They reported on impressions and clicks while the principal quietly wondered where the actual clients were.

Our founder had been inside a professional practice. He knew the difference between a growth strategy and a growth-flavoured proposal. He knew what it costs to build a practice — the years of study, the professional obligations, the reputational risk attached to every public decision. And he knew the principal deserved better than what the market was offering.

Twenty years on, Practice Proof works exclusively with law firms, healthcare practices and financial services businesses. We built our own software for it. We trained our senior team in it. We developed AI infrastructure specifically for it. The narrow focus isn't a limitation — it's the entire point.

Dan Toombs
Founder

Former lawyer. Growth strategist. 20 years inside professional practice.

Dan Toombs founded Practice Proof after practising law and seeing the gap between what professional practices needed to grow and what the marketing industry actually sold them.

He is a Winston Churchill Fellow, a recipient of the National Human Rights Law Award, and an Australia Leadership Award recipient — recognition for serious work that tells you the kind of operator he is. He is certified in both StoryBrand and Belbin Team Roles, two of the most rigorous frameworks available for narrative strategy and team dynamics.

Dan works directly with Practice Proof clients. He is in the strategy sessions, the Quarterly Success Plan reviews and the AI builds. Not a figurehead — a practitioner.
