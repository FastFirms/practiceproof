Why it matters who builds your marketing

We know where law firms lose matters — because we've been inside the firm.

The enquiry comes in at 6pm and no one answers until tomorrow, by which time they've called the next firm on Google. The intake sits in a paralegal's inbox while a conflict check waits. The Google Ads spend climbs while the cost-per-matter stays a mystery. The good clients and the tyre-kickers get the same response, because no one has the time to triage.

A generalist agency doesn't see any of this. They see a website and a media budget. We see a firm leaking matters at exactly the points where most lawyers have stopped noticing, because they've normalised the leak.

We also understand what a generalist agency will quietly get you in trouble for: the solicitors' conduct rules, the law society advertising regulations that differ across NSW, QLD, VIC and WA, and the line between confident marketing and a compliance breach. We market law firms aggressively and compliantly. Both, not one.

The six-layer system

Six layers, built for the way law firms actually win work.

  • People — Belbin mapping of your partners and senior team, because a firm divided at the top never markets with one voice.
  • Brand Strategy — positioning that separates your firm from every other personal injury, family or commercial practice claiming the same three things.
  • Brand — a StoryBrand narrative that makes the client the hero and your firm the trusted guide, across every touchpoint from website to first call.
  • Design — the credibility signal that tells a prospective client you're the firm worth trusting before they read a word.
  • Marketing — SEO, AI search, Google Ads and content that bring the right matters, measured to cost-per-qualified-matter, not clicks.
  • AI — qualification and intake that capture every matter, day or night, and route it cleanly to the right lawyer.
AI & Automation

AI that captures the matter your firm is currently losing at 6pm.

AI Lead Qualification Agent

Lives on your website, Google Business Profile and social channels. Qualifies every enquiry against jurisdiction, matter type, budget, urgency and conflict flags — before it reaches a lawyer's desk. Filters tyre-kickers, books qualified consultations straight into Practice Appointments, and syncs to PracticeDash and PracticeStripe.

AI Voice Intake

After-hours and overflow voice agent that runs first-touch intake, captures the matter facts, performs a preliminary conflict check, and routes to the right lawyer the next morning with a draft retainer already generated. The 6pm enquiry becomes a matter instead of a missed call.

Firm-Trained Claude Workspace

A private AI workspace trained on your precedents, matter library, correspondence style and procedural rules. For drafting, research and client correspondence. Isolated, IP-protected, yours alone.

AEO/GEO Search Layer

When a prospective client asks ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity or Google's AI Overviews for a lawyer in your area and practice, your firm appears. Most firms are invisible here. This is where the next decade of legal search is going.
