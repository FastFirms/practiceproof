The core builds

Six builds. One integrated AI layer.

Each build solves a specific constraint. Together they form a compounding AI infrastructure for your practice.

Build 01

AI Lead Qualification Agent

Deployed on your website, Google Business Profile and social channels. Qualifies every enquiry against your specific criteria before it reaches a desk. Filters wrong-fit enquiries, books qualified prospects into Practice Appointments, and syncs to PracticeDash and PracticeStripe.

Build 02

AI Voice Intake

After-hours and overflow voice agent. Runs first-touch intake, captures the facts, performs preliminary checks, routes to the right person the next morning, and generates draft documentation. The enquiry you used to lose becomes a client.

Build 03

Practice-Trained Claude / GPT Workspace

A private AI workspace trained on your documents, templates, precedents and brand voice. For drafting, research, correspondence and internal communications. Per-practice, isolated, IP-protected.

Build 04

AEO / GEO Search Layer

Visibility inside ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews. When a prospective client asks an AI assistant for a recommendation in your category and location, your practice appears.

Build 05

n8n Automation Stack

Connects intake, CRM, calendar and marketing. Removes the manual handoff. The lead that used to sit in someone's inbox now moves through a defined workflow automatically.

Build 06

AI-Powered Content Engine

Practice-specific content at volume — thought leadership, FAQs, guides, social — with research integrity, citation discipline and consistent brand voice. Feeds SEO, AEO, email and social at once.
