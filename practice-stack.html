Why we built it

Off-the-shelf tools are built for everyone, which means they're optimised for no one. Over 20 years we mapped exactly where professional practices leak growth — then built software to close each gap.

This stack is the reason our work compounds, and it's the part of Practice Proof a competitor can't replicate by next quarter.

Tool 01

PracticeDash

Lead tracking & performance intelligence

Know exactly where every lead came from, what happened to it, and what it was worth. PracticeDash connects marketing activity to intake outcomes, so the reporting that matters is revenue-linked, not traffic-linked. You finally see your true cost per new client, by channel.

Key capabilities

  • Lead source to revenue attribution
  • Channel-level cost per acquisition
  • Real-time intake pipeline
  • Marketing performance dashboard
